## Energy asset valuation

*MSc thesis, Boğaziçi University, 2024 · Abdulaziz Aldoseri*

Developed and evaluated a reinforcement-learning approach to natural-gas storage valuation for investment and capacity-leasing decisions. The model examines operating flexibility: adjusting buying, storage and selling as prices change, under capacity limits and operating costs.

**Evaluation:** The SAC policy learned during training but did not generalize to the 2023 validation data. This result highlights the limits of applying the learned policy outside its training period.

**Methods:** Reinforcement learning, sequential decisions, Python.

[Read the thesis (PDF)](/files/MS_thesis/Abdulaziz_Aldoseri_Thesis.pdf)

## Routing and logistics modelling

*Graduate coursework, Heuristic Optimization, Boğaziçi University · Abdulaziz Aldoseri*

Applied an Ant Colony System heuristic to the Travelling Salesman Problem with Profits, selecting routes to balance collected profit against travel cost. The C++ project reports solutions for six coursework instances.

**Evaluation:** The report records objective values and runtimes for the test instances. These are experimental results, without a verified baseline comparison or proof of optimality.

**Methods:** Optimization, Ant Colony System, C++.

[Code, instances and authored report (RAR)](/files/TSPP_ACS/TSPP.rar) · [Assignment brief (PDF)](/files/TSPP_ACS/Project.pdf)

The authored report is `Abdulaziz-Aldoseri-project-report.pdf` inside the archive.

## Simulation and uncertainty

### Fishing-policy simulation

*Coauthored graduate coursework, Boğaziçi University, 2022 · Arda Gonenc and Abdulaziz Aldoseri*

Coauthored a system-dynamics study of dusky grouper population recovery under alternative fishing policies. The Vensim model compares fishing scenarios across five life stages.

**Finding:** Within the model, protecting mature females supported recovery, while fishing that targeted them could lead to collapse. These are simulated outcomes under simplified ecological assumptions.

[Report and Vensim model (RAR)](/files/SD/Project.rar)

### Cooperation under different conditions

*Graduate group coursework, Boğaziçi University, 2022–23 · Abdulaziz Aldoseri, Tuğba Uyar and Kerem Tekeli*

Coauthored an agent-based study of cooperation in a synthetic trading population. The NetLogo simulation examines how payoffs, strategy changes and noise affect strategy prevalence and wealth outcomes.

**Finding:** Results varied with assumptions and experiment settings. Some outcomes may require longer simulation runs; the study cautions against generalizing its results to observed human behaviour.

[Report and NetLogo model (RAR)](/files/ABM/GroupD.rar)
